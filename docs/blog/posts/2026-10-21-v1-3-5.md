# AgentHub 1.3.5 — Deleted sessions and projects

Sessions and projects that agents or you deleted no longer disappear from the tool window.

<!-- more -->

## Deleted sessions

- **Codex CLI** sessions without a rollout file are listed from the Codex state database: title, first prompt, prompt count,
  model, tokens and tool calls where recorded. They are marked **Database** and still resume.
- **Claude Code** deletes transcripts after 30 days by default. Those sessions are listed from `history.jsonl`
  with their prompts and times, marked **History**. They cannot be resumed.
  Set `cleanupPeriodDays` in `~/.claude/settings.json` to keep transcripts longer.

## Projects and empty sessions

- Projects whose directory no longer exists are marked **(deleted)**, and the status line counts them.
- **Settings > Tools > AgentHub > Behavior** has two new options: **Show deleted projects** and **Show empty sessions**.
- Sessions without a prompt show as *Empty session* for every agent. Cursor, Junie and Freebuff no longer skip them.

## Resume

- Resume warns when the session is already open in a running process.
- Resume tab titles are one line and at most 30 characters.
- The info strip under the Sessions and Environment tables shows at most 5 lines.

## Get it

Install from the [JetBrains Marketplace](https://plugins.jetbrains.com/plugin/32310-agenthub) or
search for **AgentHub** in **Settings/Preferences > Plugins > Marketplace**.
